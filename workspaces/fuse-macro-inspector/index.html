How to interpret

A continuous metallic bridge in one photo versus a dark discontinuity in the other is evidence consistent with a blown fuse. Switch each image between Original, CLAHE and Sobel edge views; adjust the region sliders to align the same feature. On each panel, tap two endpoints to measure their spacing in original image pixels. Enhancement creates contrast artifacts: always switch back to Original before concluding anything.

A photograph does not prove electrical continuity. Verify with a multimeter on the removed, unpowered fuse. Never bypass a fuse or increase its amperage.
