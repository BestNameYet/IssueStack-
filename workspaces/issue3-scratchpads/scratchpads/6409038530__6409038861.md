### R4: common structure and exceptions
Common enforced linkage is the stable `ISSUESTACK_PAD_JOB:<job-id>` marker plus a per-job URL and nonce, with the index mapping. The Markdown sections `## Working notes`, `## Pre-change handshake`, `## Validation` and subsequent date-stamped headings are human conventions rather than a strict parser-generated notation schema. Some pads are boilerplate only; others contain lengthy multi-stage execution diaries, JSON review manifests, CI run IDs, hashes, source URLs, R references, W references, corrections and integrated reports. Events do *not* automatically produce pad annotations: authoring, timing, omission and detail are operator-dependent. No causal logging guarantee or one-to-one event↔entry guarantee follows from the observed documents.
